<h2>Character Creation</h2>
<hr>
<br>1 – Choose your ancestry/race, as well as a job/calling appropriate to the setting of your session. Both can be provided by your GM.
<br><b>Example ancestries:</b> Human/Lizard/Roach/Dwarf/Ogre/Elf
<br><b>Example jobs:</b> Botanist/Chef/Security Officer/Farmer/Cowboy/Space Cowboy
<br>
<br>2 – Rank your attributes.<br>
Apply one of each of the following modifiers to an attribute listed below.
<br><b>+3, +2, +1, 0, -1, -2</b>
<br><b>Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma.</b>
<br>
<br>3 – Rank your archetypes.<br>
Apply one of each of the following modifiers to an archetype listed below.
<br><b>+3, +2, +1, 0, -1, -2</b><br>
<b>Fighter, Rogue, Explorer, Sage, Artist, Diplomat.</b>
<br>
<br>4 – Give yourself 10 points of resolve. How resolve works is explained in “Playing the game”
<br>
<br>5 – Choose a name and appearance.
<br>
<br>6 – Start with whatever basic gear makes sense for your race and job assignment.<br>
<br>Basic gear identifies as a set or a collection of minor tools and items. For example, someone with an engineering assignment could plausibly possess a belt of tools, fire extinguisher and similar items. Similarly, a hunter-gathered could plausibly possess a hatchet, bow, and trapping tools. The plausibility and limit of what items you can possess should be consulted with your GM. You can carry 3 significant items at once.

<h2>Playing The Game</h2>
<hr>
<br>A – Start a quest!<br>
<br>B – To take an action, describe what you want to do and then – if the GM asks – make a <b>Success Check</b> against a <b>Difficulty Class</b>. If you fail, the GM can make a reaction against you.<br>
<br><b>Success Check = (1d20 +  Attribute rank +  Archetype rank)</b>
<br>vs
<br><b>Difficulty Class = 1 d5/d10/d15/d20/d25/d30</b><br>
<br>C – If something attacks or acts against you, make a success check to defend.<br>
<br>D – If you suffer harm, you lose 1 resolve. If you deal harm, your target loses 1 resolve.<br>
<br>E – You can spend 1 resolve to roll with advantage or attempt an extraordinary feat.<br>
<br>F – If you have 0 resolve, you are knocked out. You gain 1 resolve when you finish a long rest.<br>
<br>G – When the quest is resolved, the game ends! If you survived, gain a perk: Choose either +1  Attribute point, +1 Archetype point or +1 maximum resolve.<br>
<br><br>
This ruleset is entirely based upon the Quick Quest system, with minor changes. The source material is made by GiffyGlyph, which can be found <a href="https://giffyglyph.com/quickquest" target="_blank">here</a>.
